# P-52 Remove the retention setting

Decision: there is no data retention for now. Nothing is deleted by age (P-08 is retired). Tickets, screenshots, replays, comments, and the activity log are kept until someone deletes them by hand. The `reports_retention_days` setting is a control that does nothing, so it is removed instead of being left as a false promise.

This is a removal task: no new behavior.

## Scope

Remove `reports_retention_days` everywhere:

- **UI** (`apps/web/src/client/modules/organization/`): the Retention section in `components/organization-settings-form.tsx` (editable and read-only variants), `consts/organization-retention-day.const.ts`, the retention options and field in `hooks/use-organization-settings-form.ts`, the field in `schemas/organization-settings.schema.ts`, and the `retention` texts in `consts/texts.const.ts` (EN and RU). The Organization tab keeps only the name section, and the Save button and the "Saved" indicator stay in that section.
- **DTOs** (`packages/validation/src/modules/organization/dto/`): `organization.model.ts`, `find-organizations.schema.ts`, `create-organization.schema.ts`, `update-organization.schema.ts`. `update-organization` now requires `name`.
- **Server** (`packages/server/src/modules/organization/`): entity, types, factory (and its 90-day default), mapper (`toAudit`, `toDto`, `toFindOrganizationsOutput`), repository insert and upsert, `create-organization.use-case.ts`, `update-organization.use-case.ts`. OpenAPI example in `packages/server/src/shared/openapi/schema-registry.ts`.
- **Database:** drop the column from `packages/database/src/schema/organization.const.ts` (and the unused `integer` import), update `packages/database/src/seed/organization.ts`, and add one Drizzle migration in `packages/database/src/migration` that drops `organizations.reports_retention_days`. Do not edit old migrations or snapshots by hand.
- **Tests:** drop the retention assertions and fixtures from `create-organization.test.ts`, `update-organization.test.ts` (the test "updates the organization retention period" becomes "updates the organization name" if it does not exist yet), `find-organizations.test.ts`, `find-organization-by-id.test.ts`, `find-onboarding.test.ts`, `update-onboarding-organization.test.ts`, and `packages/server/src/modules/invitation/application/send-invitation.test.ts`. e2e `e2e/organization/update-organization.spec.ts`: rename to "an administrator saves the organization name" and remove the Retention steps.

Do not add any deletion job, cron route, or `CRON_SECRET`.
